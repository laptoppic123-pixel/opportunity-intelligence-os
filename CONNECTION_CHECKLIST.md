4. **Authorized finite research credits.** `configs/sources.yaml` records owner-declared starting allowances, not verified balances. Tavily: 1,000 included basic-search credits, one conservative credit hold per attempt. Exa: approximately $20 promotional credit; only $19 is locally authorized, leaving a $1 buffer. Each bounded auto search (at most five results, highlights only) holds $0.02 against that promotional allowance. Gross provider usage is identified by credit type and is not reported as a verified card charge. Failed or unknown attempts retain conservative credit holds. The database atomically denies exhausted local allowances; credit/access failures stop the affected provider. Paid access remains OFF. Neither declaration renews automatically, and both stop at the fixed local expiry in configuration. Replenishment requires another explicit owner declaration; rotating a key does not replenish credits. No balance polling, scraping fallback, purchases, auto-top-ups or paid fallback is implemented. Exa extraction and other optional paid operations remain blocked without separate reviewed pricing and authorization.
